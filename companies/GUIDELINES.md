# Companies

This document will explain in detail what are companies in CRSS and how do they work. Keep in mind, they're mainly for RP, but we take these things quite seriously... Perhaps too seriously.

## What are companies in CRSS?

Companies are all businesses in CRSS that want to be registered as "official". Of course, you're not *forced* to do this, but having your company be on the repo means it's easier to have it known by people, and perhaps be featured in CRSS' YouTube videos.

You can see the list of companies in CRSS [here](https://github.com/CRSS666/CRSS/tree/crss/Companies).

## How do I start my own?

It's completely free to make your own company! Not even diamonds are involved.

To start, [fork the CRSS repo](https://github.com/CRSS666/CRSS/fork), and make sure you follow the standard style.

## Standard style

Companies have a few requirements to be able to be accepted into the main repo:

- All branding must be done in [Vector Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_graphics) (SVG)
- All companies must be under a subfolder in their nation of origin
- All companies must have a README, even if only to describe minimal stuff (such as which nation they originated from, what they offer, etc.)
  - The README should consist of:
    - Company Name (Title)
    - Company description (Body)
    - Owners (Title, followed by owners in a list)
    - Date of creation

As an example, check [Blurbank Inc.](https://github.com/CRSS666/CRSS/tree/crss/Companies/Republic%20of%20Panorama/Blurbank%20Inc):

![Blurbank Inc.](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9cb66ef7-7073-4c1f-9071-3baf6eafca00)

Company names should remain appropriate, and may have suffixes such as "Inc.", "Corp.", "Co." etc.

## How do I get my company into the main repo?

After you're done with the changes in your fork, you can create a pull request to request that your changes get added into the main repo.

![Create Pull Request](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/30b9298c-5344-47c3-8628-124528ee1fd0)

The admins will take a look at your changes, and may deny, request changes, or approve it.

After getting approved, your changes will get merged to the main repo, and your company is officially live! 
Do make sure to have a physical place for it, though.
